New Haven, 2 °C air at a −1 °C dew point, heated to 20.5 °C indoors.

9 of 16 cities land in a different advice band once the indoor figure is computed. Mean gap 25.6 points; largest 58.6.

  • The dew point is the temperature where air saturates and water condenses — an absolute measure of the water actually in the air.
  • Relative humidity is a percentage of what the air could hold. Heat the air and that capacity rises, so the percentage collapses — with no water removed.
  • This is why skin cracks in winter while the forecast says "humid."

What is new

  • Computes indoor humidity from the dew point. Weather apps stop at the outdoor figure. Skincare apps treat weather as static. No consumer product does this calculation.
  • Treats skin and clothing as one problem. Occlusive balm stains silk. Chemical SPF yellows white cotton. Mineral SPF casts on dark collars. The product on your face determines the fabric that should touch it.
  • Knows how old your products are. A pasted receipt dates every item — surfacing oxidised actives and out-of-season buys.
  • Looks ahead. Barrier care takes days to build, so it warns before the air dries — Seoul: 51% to 17% in two days.
  • Tested against ChatGPT directly. Asked the way a person would ask, it gives different advice in 6 of 16 cities — worst miss 46 points, where it simply handed back the outdoor number.
  • Will not recommend a purchase without the money attached. Every suggestion resolves to use what you have, replace, or genuinely new — shown beside this month's spend.

The agents

city ─▶ weather.py Open-Meteo · no API key · offline fallback ▼ climate.py psychrometrics — every number computed here ▼ guard.py injection screen · medical boundary · caps │ ┌─────┴─────┐ ▼ ▼ Shelf Reader Stylist use/skip/gap outfit/avoid └─────┬─────┘ ▼ Briefer → _enforce_invariants()

No agent does arithmetic. Every number is computed in Python first. That removes confident-wrong-numbers by construction, not by prompting.

The one direct test of behaviour change came back negative. People not knowing the number is measured. Telling them helping is not — and that is now the first thing to settle, not the last.

Honest limit: five people, asked once, by someone they know. Enough to motivate the product, far too small to prove anything about a population.

Limits and next steps

Limits

  • Indoor temperature is assumed (20.5 °C), not measured — the largest error source.
  • A humidifier invalidates the estimate entirely.
  • Thresholds are defensible, not validated.
  • Purchase date stands in for opening date.

Next

  • Photograph the shelf instead of pasting a receipt.
  • Ask indoor temperature once, or read a thermostat.
  • 10 users × 4 weeks to validate the bands.
  • Move the Briefer off a reasoning model.
