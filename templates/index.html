The humidity on your weather app is outside. Your skin is inside — where heating strips the air in winter and air conditioning wrings it out in summer. Either way it is nothing like the number you were shown.

DewPoint works out the humidity your skin is actually in, then plans your routine and your outfit around it. Across 16 cities, 9 of them land in a different advice band once you compute it.
